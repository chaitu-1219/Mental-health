🧠 Mental Health Prediction in Tech Industry
📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing mental health conditions in the tech industry and predicting whether an employee is likely to seek treatment based on various personal and workplace factors.

Mental health is a growing concern in modern workplaces. This project uses Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Machine Learning techniques to uncover insights and support organizations in improving employee well-being.

🎯 Problem Statement

Mental health issues in the tech industry often go unnoticed due to stigma, lack of awareness, and insufficient support systems. This project aims to analyze employee survey data and build a predictive model to identify individuals who may require mental health support.

🎯 Business Objective
Identify employees who may need mental health support
Understand factors influencing mental health
Improve workplace wellness programs
Reduce productivity loss
Promote a supportive work environment
